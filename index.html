Rich Culture

Bihar is home to diverse traditions, folk dances like Jat-Jatin and Bidesia, and festivals such as Chhath Puja, Teej, and Holi. The state's literature and crafts showcase its timeless cultural essence.

Traditional Cuisine

  • Litti Chokha – Roasted wheat balls stuffed with sattu and served with mashed vegetables.
  • Thekua – A sweet snack made during Chhath Puja.
  • Khaja – A crispy, layered dessert from Silao.
